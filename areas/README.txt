Examples for the Homes and Income layers of Your Place · Data
==============================================================

freiburg-rent-2022.csv
  Average net rent per m² (without heating) of rented homes, in 100 m squares, for 6 × 6 km
  around the centre of Freiburg im Breisgau, cut unchanged from the 2022 census grid data
  "Durchschnittliche Nettokaltmiete in Gitterzellen".
  Source: Zensus 2022, Statistische Ämter des Bundes und der Länder,
  https://www.destatis.de/zensus2022
  Licence: Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung – Version 2.0 (dl-de/by-2-0),
  https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/by-2-0
  Changes: only the rows for this area are kept.

amsterdam-income-cbs.geojson
  Average income per resident and per income earner (in €1,000 a year) for the districts
  (wijken) of central Amsterdam, from "Kerncijfers wijken en buurten 2023".
  Source: Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek (CBS), via PDOK, https://www.cbs.nl/
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: only these districts and four of their figures are kept; water areas are left out;
  coordinates are rounded to 6 decimals.

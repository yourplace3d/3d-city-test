# 3dcitydata

**Your Place · Data**: open data on a 3D model of any place. The twin of
[Your Place](https://yourplace3d.github.io/3d-city/): Your Place turns an address into a 3D model to
share, hang on the wall or 3D print; its twin lays data over that model.

**Open it:** https://yourplace3d.github.io/3dcitydata/data/

- **Steepness:** roads and paths coloured by how steep they are, anywhere.
- **Traffic (Hamburg):** cars and bikes counted every hour, as red streets that rise with the
  traffic, live from the city's open data.
- **Your route:** load a GPX or TCX file of a run, ride or race, pick the stretch, and get it as a
  poster or a 3D print, for example to frame with your medal. The file stays on your device.

More data layers are on the way, such as water and energy.

Built files only. The source is in [yourplace3d/3d-city](https://github.com/yourplace3d/3d-city)
(branch `v2`), under the MIT licence.

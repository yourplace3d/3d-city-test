# Your Place · Data

**Open data about your city on a 3D model**: how steep its streets are, how busy, how its trams and buses
run, what homes cost and what people earn. Search any place, pick a layer. Free, no account, works on
your phone.

**Open it:** https://yourplace3d.github.io/3dcitydata/

- **Steepness:** roads and paths coloured by how steep they are, anywhere.
- **Traffic (Hamburg):** cars and bikes counted every hour, as red streets that rise with the traffic, live
  from the city's open data.
- **Public transport:** trams, buses, trains and ferries moving through the square as your city's timetable
  (GTFS) has them; find it in the panel or load the file.
- **Homes and Income:** the buildings coloured by rents, home values or incomes per area, from open data
  (Germany's census, CBS for the Netherlands, the US census via Census Reporter) or a file of your own.
- **Your route:** a GPX or TCX file of a run, ride or race over any layer, with its climbs, height profile
  and replay.

Files you load are read in your browser and never uploaded.

Built files only. The source is in [yourplace3d/3d-city](https://github.com/yourplace3d/3d-city/tree/v2)
(branch `v2`), under the MIT licence.
